Get church announcements and bulletins sent straight to your phone — no app install, no data costs.

On iPhone, you must add this page to your Home Screen first:
  1. Tap the Share button (⬆️ square icon) at the bottom
  2. Choose "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Open the app from the new icon on your Home Screen
  4. Then tap "Enable Notifications" again
This page does not store any personal information beyond your notification subscription.
